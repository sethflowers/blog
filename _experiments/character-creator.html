CharacterCreator

Design a person, photo-real or cartoon: every part of the face and body, skin, hair and clothes. Save them, and take them to another device or game with a code.

 

iPad: drag the person to turn them · pinch to zoom · tabs on the left, sliders on the right · double-tap a slider to reset it
Computer: drag to turn · wheel to zoom · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo
VR: point and pull the trigger at the board · hold the trigger to drag a slider · grip turns the person · left stick walks · B/Y hides the board
