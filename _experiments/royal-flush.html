Royal Flush

The great Magic Kingdom restroom quest.
Find them all. Rate them all. Wash your hands.

How to play: walk to a 🚻 pin and tap Found it! Being there with GPS on earns a GPS bonus. Then scrub your hands for 20 seconds for more points, and rate the restroom.
🚨 I need to go! shows the nearest restroom and the way there.
Finish a land for a bonus. Find them all for a Royal Flush.

An unofficial fan-made game, not connected with Disney. The map is redrawn from the park guide and lined up with OpenStreetMap (map data © OpenStreetMap contributors). Pins can be a few steps off: when you find one, you can move its pin to where you're standing.
