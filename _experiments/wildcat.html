WILDCATalone in the wild

You are a wildcat, small and fierce, in a forest that goes on and on. Brown bears, grey wolves and wild boar live here too. Get too close and they will come for you, and they won't let you run: you have to fight. Catch rabbits to get your strength back.

Controller left stick to move (click it to sprint), right stick to look, X or RT claw, A pounce, B dodge.
Keyboard WASD to move, Shift to sprint, mouse to look, click to claw, Space to pounce, right-click or Q to dodge. Left thumb moves (push to the edge to sprint), right thumb looks. Buttons on the right claw, pounce and dodge.
