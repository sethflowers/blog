UNDERCROFTfree them · find the way up

People are locked in cells deep under the ground. Zombies wander the dark, and something very tall lives in the great hall. Walk through the power gates to take their powers. Deep water is death. You have five hearts; eat to get them back.

Controller left stick walks (click to hurry), right stick looks, RT attack, LT power, LB swap power, RB swap weapon, A open / break a lock (hold), X eat, Y bag, Start pause.
Keyboard WASD, mouse to look, click attack, right-click or Q power, R swap power, 1–4 weapons, E use, F eat, Tab bag, Shift hurry. Left thumb walks (push to the edge to hurry), right thumb looks. Buttons on the right attack, use powers, open things and eat.
