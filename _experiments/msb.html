Your own art gallery. Take photos with your real camera, turn them into art, and sell them!

Make: 📷 takes a real photo · edit it with crop, filters, sliders, drawing, stickers, words and frames
Sell: hang it on a wall and visitors make offers, or sell it to the dealer
Buy: the 🛒 Art Market desk sells paintings, frames, decorations and a whole new wing
iPad: left thumb walks · drag to look · tap frames, people and the desk
Computer: WASD walks · drag to look · click things · C camera · I my art · B buy
VR: left stick walks · right stick turns · A camera · trigger takes the picture or picks things · X menu · B closes
