DOTTIE’SMIX AND MATCH RACER

Build a racer out of parts from different animals, then race it. Every part changes how your animal runs, jumps, corners and swims. Some combinations are brilliant. Some are a disaster.
Seventeen animals, five hilly tracks, and coins scattered all over every one of them.

Your racer accelerates on its own, so all you do is steer. Put your thumb down anywhere on the left of the screen and slide it — the further you slide, the harder you turn. Save the boost for the straights.

Spend your coins on new animals, new tracks, or furniture for the Animal House, which you can walk around inside.
