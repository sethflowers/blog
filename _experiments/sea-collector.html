Sea Collector

You are a scuba diver with a little house at the bottom of the sea. Swim out, collect shells, and bring them home before your air runs out.

Watch out for sharks, jellyfish, eels and spiky urchins — and the monsters that climb out of the sand every once in a while (look out for the Mega Monster!). Catch them with your net gun 🕸️, give a dolphin a shell to make a friend 🐬, and spend your shells at home on new clothes and better scuba gear, then dive deeper.
