GLUEGUNNER

A deck floating in the sky. Ten aliens. One glue gun.
Stick them to the floating blocks and rip them apart.

 

iPad: left thumb walks · drag to look · tap something to shoot it · 1st shot sticks a string, 2nd shot sticks the other end · ✂ cuts
Computer: WASD to walk · drag to look · click or Space to shoot · C cuts every string · Esc: menu
VR: a glue gun in each hand (triggers) · grip cuts · left stick walks · right stick turns · X/Y: menu
